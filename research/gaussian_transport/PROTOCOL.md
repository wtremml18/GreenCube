# Exploratory Gaussian transport and fluid checks

These experiments were added after the initial track experiments and after discovering LagrangianSplats (SIGGRAPH 2026). They are exploratory, not preregistered. No image reconstruction performance is claimed.

1. Twenty analytic 3D field/noise seeds, 48 direct velocity samples, solenoidal ABC-like flow and the same flow plus isotropic expansion. Joint RK4 integration of positions and deformation gradients transports small Gaussian covariances. Compare divergence-free, independent-component and common-length Helmholtz kernels with training-only four-fold tuning.
2. Before inspecting fluid prediction errors: use ScalarSyn frames 0, 10, 20, 29 from the checksum-verified public LagrangianSplats ground-truth archive. Follow the upstream crop to (z,y,x)=(100,150,100). Coordinates are voxel indices divided by 100 on all axes, preserving isotropy. Keep vector components in source units; no physical-time calibration is asserted. Five sampling/noise seeds per frame; 96 direct velocity observations; 512 independent smoke-volume test voxels (density >1% of frame maximum, interior).
3. Compare three equal-budget designs: four vertical lines at x,z in {0.3,0.7}, 24 samples per line; replace eight of those 96 samples with randomly selected smoke-volume points; 96 randomly selected smoke-volume points. All methods use the same observations, noise (1% of training component RMS), and test points within a design. The target density mask is available geometry, not velocity labels. Remove all test voxels from random training pools. Four-fold random training CV selects lengths {0.08,0.16,0.32,0.64}, ridge {0.001,0.03,0.3}, and mixture {0,.25,.5,.75,1}. Report raw vector MSE and normalization by test velocity energy. These frames belong to one simulation and are not independent events.
4. Check the scalar-density aperture ambiguity separately. For smooth density rho, w=grad(rho) cross a constant vector is divergence-free and tangent to density level sets. Adding w cannot change the instantaneous advective derivative. Demonstrate the discrete identity on public density volumes, and an exact full-time ambiguity using an analytic anisotropic Gaussian density and affine flows preserving that density. This is a diagnostic of established nonidentifiability, not a claim to have discovered the aperture problem or a full fluid-dynamics symmetry. Momentum, boundary conditions, texture or direct velocity measurements can restrict it.

The fluid experiment supplies velocity observations that video-based reconstruction does not directly have. It tests interpolation under realistic flow structure, not the complete LagrangianSplats pipeline. No PSNR, novel-view rendering, or superiority to that publication is inferred.

Post-outcome control: initial lines at x,z={.3,.7} covered very little visible smoke (0%,0%,4.2%,8.3% over the four frames). Consequently their large improvement after adding smoke-volume samples conflates spatial coverage with support coverage. Preserve the initial result and add `--support-matched`: choose fixed lines x,z={.45,.55}, take 24 approximately equally spaced eligible y voxels on each line within the same density mask, and exclude these from test/random training pools. Each design then has 100% smoke-support observations. Use the same physical noise SD across all three designs, 1% of the component RMS over the masked full source volume, and the same standard-normal draws. This source truth is used only to generate controlled simulated measurement noise; the estimator and tuning never receive target velocities. Report this control as the principal fluid sampling result, and retain the unfavorable/confounded initial result for transparency.
